# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployed on Netlify (anfaweddings.com). Existing codebase; keep it plain static.

## Users

Brides and couples planning a wedding in Belgium and nearby, with a focus on Moroccan and Maghrebi brides: caftan looks, Ziana (the Moroccan bridal styling and adornment tradition), multi-outfit celebrations. Couples of every background are also welcome. They arrive mostly from Instagram on a phone, comparing photographers, and want to know quickly whether this photographer understands their kind of wedding and how to ask about a date.

## Product Purpose

The site of ANFA Weddings, a wedding and bridal photographer based in Belgium who also travels. It shows the work and turns interest into an inquiry. Success is a visitor sending a request through WhatsApp or Instagram.

## Positioning

Editorial, sincere photography for weddings, brides and Ziana, with real fluency in Moroccan bridal craft: embroidery, caftans, jewellery, and the detail of the looks, alongside natural, relaxed wedding-day storytelling.

## Operating Context

- Inquiries go to WhatsApp (+32 472 73 83 07). The form builds a pre-filled WhatsApp message the visitor reviews and sends; there is no backend.
- Instagram: @anfaweddings (weddings) and @anfavisuals (sister account).
- Three languages: French (default), Dutch, English, switched in the page and remembered per visitor.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: wedding photography; bridal and Ziana portraits; creative collaborations for designers, artists and wedding professionals.
- The current copy in all three languages is confirmed and stays as written.
- Only four photographs are available for now (hero plus three portfolio images); the layout must work with them and accept more later.
- Undecided: pricing, packages, availability calendar. None of these appear on the site.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: ANFA Weddings. The existing black "Anfa WEDDINGS" logo (images/logo.png) stays.
- Voice: warm, first person, sincere, elegant; "images that feel like you".

## Product Principles

- The photographs are the proof; everything else serves them.
- Respect the craft of the looks: embroidery, fabric and jewellery are subjects, not backgrounds.
- Asking about a date should take seconds, on a phone, in the visitor's language.
- Inclusive of every couple, fluent in Moroccan celebration.
